## 1. Executive Summary & The GTM Thesis

Scaling food delivery in secondary (tier-2) Nigerian cities like Ilorin requires an acquisition and retention playbook fundamentally different from tier-1 capitals (Lagos, Abuja, Nairobi). Lagos features 6+ well-funded venture-backed delivery platforms burning millions of dollars in Google Ads and discount subsidies to win high-income, dense residential corridors. 

In contrast, Ilorin represents a largely greenfield, underserved market where platform loyalty is driven by **operational trust, hot delivery execution, and WhatsApp-native simplicity**, not unsustainable venture-funded discount wars.

### The Three Founding Assumptions
1. **Ilorin is Winnable and Defensible:** Middle-class disposable income in Ilorin is concentrated in distinct, geographically compact corridors (Tanke student & lecturer housing, GRA corporate estates, Fate residential zones). Winning Ilorin builds a cashflow-positive regional monopoly that serves as the template for tier-2 expansion (Osogbo, Ado-Ekiti, Akure).
2. **The Customer is WhatsApp-Native:** The average Nigerian smartphone owner faces prepaid mobile data constraints and storage limits. They do not want to download a 65MB APK to order dinner twice a week. We bring the store to where they already spend 3+ hours daily: **WhatsApp**.
3. **Trust and Convenience Trump Price Wars:** A customer happily pays ₦700 for delivery if their jollof rice arrives in 28 minutes piping hot and they can pay with an instant bank transfer. Subsidizing delivery fees to ₦200 attracts price-sensitive deal-hunters who churn the moment market pricing returns.

### The 6-Month "Proven Model" Milestone
We do not expand outside of Ilorin until these unit economic gates are achieved:
* **5,000 Monthly Active Customers (MAC)** in Ilorin.
* **$\ge 30\%$ Month-over-Month Repeat Order Rate**.
* **Blended CAC $< ₦1,400** per acquired customer.
* **LTV:CAC Ratio $> 3.5:1$** at the 6-month horizon.
* **Contribution Margin Positive ($+₦260$ to $+₦500$)** on every single delivered order.

### Segment Details

#### Segment A: Higher Education Students (Priority 1 — Volume Driver)
* **Population Base:** $\sim 40,000$ students across University of Ilorin (Unilorin Main Campus & PS) and Kwara State Polytechnic.
* **Housing Geography:** Dense private hostel clusters along Tanke Akata, Tanke Oke-Odo, Sanrab, and Tipper Garage.
* **Ordering Behavior:** Late-night study cravings ($21:00\text{--}01:00$ WAT), shawarma, peppered turkey, suya, and heavy carbs (Amala, Fried Rice).
* **Viral Dynamics:** Students live in high-density hostel WhatsApp groups, faculty associations, and departmental chats. One viral receipt share triggers organic peer adoption across entire hostel wings.
* **Target CAC:** **₦400 – ₦700**.

#### Segment B: Young Professionals & Civil Servants (Priority 2 — Margin Driver)
* **Population Base:** $\sim 20,000$ working adults in banking, Kwara State Government Secretariat, health sectors, and tech remote work.
* **Housing Geography:** GRA, Fate Road, Basin, and Adewole Estate.
* **Ordering Behavior:** Desk-side weekday lunch ($12:00\text{--}14:00$ WAT) and dinner after long traffic commutes. Highly appreciative of reliable delivery tracking.
* **Target CAC:** **₦800 – ₦1,200**.

#### Segment C: Family Households (Priority 3 — Basket Size Driver)
* **Population Base:** $\sim 15,000$ suburban households in GRA, Pipeline, and Offa Garage.
* **Ordering Behavior:** Friday evening comfort food, Sunday family stew/rice orders, and birthday catering. Average basket size exceeds ₦6,000.
* **Target CAC:** **₦1,000 – ₦1,500**.

#### Segment D: Corporate & Institutional Accounts (Deferred to Day 90+)
* *Rule:* Do not divert field sales to corporate procurement in the first 90 days. Focus 100% of bandwidth on high-velocity consumer orders.

### Dynamic Multipliers
* **Peak Lunch & Dinner Multiplier ($1.2\times$):** Applied $12:00\text{--}14:00$ and $18:30\text{--}21:00$ WAT.
* **Rainstorm Surcharge ($1.3\times$):** Auto-triggered when OpenWeatherMap API registers active rainfall in Ilorin (compensates riders for wet road risk).
* **Late-Night Multiplier ($1.4\times$):** Applied $22:00\text{--}01:00$ WAT for student late-night operations.

### Platform Technology Toll & Cart Thresholds
* **Platform Service Fee:** ₦100.00 flat fee per order (transparently shown on receipt).
* **Minimum Order Basket:** **₦1,000.00**. Orders below ₦1,000 cannot sustain delivery unit economics and are politely rejected at cart creation.

### Customer Payment Channel Distribution
* **65% Monnify Dynamic Virtual Accounts (Primary):** Instant bank transfer; auto-reconciled within 30 seconds.
* **20% Korapay Debit Cards:** Direct debit card processing for tech-savvy professionals.
* **15% Cash on Delivery (Restricted Fallback):** Capped at ₦5,000 per order, restricted to verified cluster residents, and scheduled to scale down to $<5\%$ by Month 6.

## 9. Referral Mechanics Adapted for Nigerian Culture

Silicon Valley referral programs (email links, browser cookies) fail in Nigeria. Our referral architecture operates through peer trust, WhatsApp Status, and symmetric incentives.

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        SYMMETRIC REFERRAL INCENTIVE MODEL                              │
├─────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Dimension           │ Operational Rule & Financial Settlement                          │
├─────────────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ Existing Referrer   │ Receives **₦500.00 platform credit** on their next food order.   │
│ New Referee         │ Receives **₦500.00 platform discount** on their first order.     │
│ Trigger Condition   │ Referee must complete and PAY for their first order ($\ge ₦1,500$│
│                     │ basket threshold). Zero credit granted for abandoned carts.      │
│ Expiry Window       │ Referral credits expire after 14 days to force urgency.          │
│ Stacking Rule       │ Only 1 referral coupon can be redeemed per checkout transaction. │
└─────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Fraud Defenses & Anti-Sybil Rules
1. **Device Fingerprinting:** Device binding engine ensures only one referral bonus is claimed per physical phone hardware.
2. **Phone Number Uniqueness:** Virtual burner VoIP numbers blocked; only real Nigerian SIMs validated via Termii pass.
3. **Account Age Gate:** Referrer must have placed at least 1 verified, paid order before unlocking a referral link.
4. **Velocity Limit:** Maximum 5 referral rewards per user per month. Accounts exceeding this are flagged for ops review.

## 12. 10 Strict Prohibitions: What NOT to Do

1. **Do Not Buy Customers with Deep Discounts:** Deep discounts ($>50\%$) attract discount hunters who leave when market pricing returns. Incentives are capped at free delivery on order #1.
2. **Do Not Launch Multiple Clusters Simultaneously:** Never open GRA until Tanke hits $70\%$ saturation. Diluting rider supply breaks delivery SLAs.
3. **No Billboard or Radio Spend in Phase 1:** Traditional mass media cannot be attributed and burns working capital. Every marketing kobo must generate an attributable WhatsApp conversation.
4. **Never Overpromise on Delivery Speed:** State 35 minutes and deliver in 28. If you advertise 20 minutes and deliver in 30, the customer feels deceived.
5. **Never Ignore Customer Complaints:** Every complaint is resolved within 4 hours. Unresolved complaints spread virally in student hostel groups.
6. **Never Match Competitor Price Cuts:** We compete on **reliability, hot food, and live tracking**, not suicidal sub-economic price wars.
7. **Never Accept Orders Outside Cluster Boundaries:** Serving customers 12km away strands riders and delays other orders. Boundaries are strict.
8. **Do Not Hire Premature Growth Marketers:** Do not expand marketing headcount until product-market fit and repeat retention are proven.
9. **Never Sacrifice Vendor Relationships for Promotions:** Forcing vendors to fund platform discounts creates resentment and degrades food portion sizes.
10. **Kill Unprofitable Campaigns Fast:** If an influencer campaign or flyer batch does not break even within 14 days, terminate it immediately.

## 22.3 Corporate Banking, Payment Rails & Trust Accounting

* **Primary Banking Partner:** Commercial account with **Guaranty Trust Bank (GTBank)** or **Access Bank** (selected for dedicated Kwara State enterprise branches, robust corporate online banking APIs, and high settlement reliability).

* **Signatory Threshold Controls:** Dual authorization (CEO + CTO/CFO) required for all outgoing electronic transfers exceeding **₦500,000 NGN**.

### Payment Aggregator Partnerships (Monnify & Korapay)
* **Monnify (Primary Virtual Account Gateway):**
  * Dynamic virtual NUBAN accounts generated per checkout transaction (`ORD-XXXX`).
  * Real-time automated credit webhooks triggering order state transitions.
  * Pricing: 1.5% transaction toll capped at ₦2,000 NGN. Next-day (T+1) automated sweep to corporate bank.
* **Korapay (Secondary Backup & Card Gateway):**
  * Redundant virtual account fallback and debit card checkout rails (Mastercard, Visa, Verve).
  * Pricing: 1.4% + ₦100 per card transaction.
* **Escrow Trust Legal Structure:** Customer food payments are legally received by Dispatch as a fiduciary agent in trust for the vendor. Monies do not constitute platform revenue until delivery fulfillment is confirmed. Funds are strictly segregated in the PostgreSQL ledger (`1100_CUSTOMER_ESCROW`) and never commingled with corporate opex.

## 22.5 Employment, Statutory Labor & Gig Worker Classification

### Full-Time Employee Statutory Burdens (14.5% Above Gross Payroll)
Nigerian labor law mandates four non-negotiable statutory contributions on full-time employee salaries:

| Statutory Scheme | Governed by | Mandatory Contribution Rate | Remittance Schedule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Contributory Pension (CPS)** | PenCom / Licensed PFA | **10.0% Employer** + 8.0% Employee | By 7th of following month |
| **National Housing Fund (NHF)**| Federal Mortgage Bank | **2.5% Basic Salary** | By 7th of following month |
| **Industrial Training Fund (ITF)**| Industrial Training Fund | **1.0% Gross Annual Payroll** | Annually (April 1st) |
| **Workplace Injury Scheme (NSITF)**| NSITF | **1.0% Gross Monthly Payroll** | By 7th of following month |
| **TOTAL EMPLOYER STATUTORY BURDEN** | — | **14.5% Above Gross Base** | Mandatory Budget Line |

### Protecting the Gig Worker Classification for Riders
To prevent catastrophic labor tribunal rulings reclassifying our 100+ motorcycle riders as full-time employees (which would trigger over **₦30M+ in retroactive back-taxes and pension claims**), rider agreements must strictly enforce:
1. **Independent Contractor Status:** Express contractual designation as an independent freelance logistics provider.
2. **Non-Exclusivity:** Riders retain full contractual liberty to perform deliveries for competing services or personal clients.
3. **Freedom of Offer Rejection:** Zero punitive administrative penalties or account suspensions for rejecting dispatch delivery offers.
4. **Tool Ownership:** Riders provide their own motorcycles, helmets, and smartphones.
5. **Absence of Fixed Hours:** Zero mandatory shift rosters or required minimum hours per week.

## 22.7 Consumer Protection, Food Safety & Intermediary Immunity

### Platform Intermediary Legal Status
Dispatch Technologies Ltd. operates strictly as an **information technology marketplace intermediary** connecting independent food canteens with consumers. We are not a food manufacturer, canteen, or common carrier:
* **NAFDAC Compliance:** While raw food prepared by informal bukkas is outside direct NAFDAC pre-packaged licensing, Dispatch requires all partner vendors to sign food safety representations.
* **Kwara State Ministry of Health:** Partner merchants must permit municipal health inspections. Dispatch maintains a digital repository of vendor health certifications.
* **FCCPC Fair Pricing Compliance:** All platform delivery charges, platform service fees, and food subtotals are explicitly itemized prior to Monnify payment presentation, barring deceptive drip-pricing patterns.

### Food Safety Incident & Contamination Protocol
In the event of a customer food poisoning report or foreign object claim:
1. **Immediate Medical Assistance:** Customer support authorizes medical triage reimbursement up to **₦200,000 NGN**.
2. **2-Hour Vendor Suspension:** The implicated merchant is immediately deactivated (`is_available = false`) pending formal review.
3. **24-Hour Municipal Notification:** Formal notification submitted to the Kwara State Ministry of Health environmental health officer.
4. **48-Hour Kitchen Audit:** Field operations team inspects the vendor's kitchen, water source, and storage facilities.
5. **Incident Escrow Reserve:** Dispatch maintains an ongoing **₦500,000 NGN quarterly reserve fund** to resolve verified contamination disputes without operational disruption.

## 22.8 Rider Fleet Regulatory Compliance & Legal Defense

1. **Driver Licensing:** All riders must present a valid **Class A Motorcycle Driver's License** issued by the Federal Road Safety Corps (FRSC).
2. **Kwara State Transport Union Accord:** Dispatch maintains formal operational engagement with local chapters of the Amalgamated Commercial Motorcycle Riders Association of Nigeria (ACOMORAN).
3. **Police Extortion & Bail Defense Reserve:** To protect riders against arbitrary municipal road blocks or harassment in secondary transit corridors, Dispatch maintains a **₦200,000 NGN cash bail float** and retains a local Ilorin litigation lawyer on monthly retainer (₦150,000/mo) to provide immediate legal intervention.

## 22.9 Commercial Vendor Services Agreement (Key Clauses)

Every food canteen executes a binding standard Vendor Agreement containing:
* **Commission Retainage:** Explicit contractual authority for Dispatch to deduct a 15%–20% commission and ₦100 technology fee from gross customer payments.
* **Settlement Schedule:** Payout disbursements executed twice weekly (Tuesdays and Fridays) via automated NIBSS Instant Payments to the merchant’s verified bank account.
* **Food Quality Indemnification:** The vendor expressly indemnifies and holds Dispatch harmless against any customer claims, illnesses, or deaths arising from contaminated or spoiled food.
* **Intellectual Property License:** The vendor grants Dispatch an irrevocable, royalty-free license to use their business name, chalkboard menu photographs, and dish trademarks across web, PWA, and marketing channels.

## 22.10 Customer Terms of Service, Privacy & Refund Policy

1. **Marketplace Disclaimer:** Customers explicitly acknowledge that Dispatch is a software platform facilitating transactions and does not cook or prepare food.
2. **Three-Tier Refund Policy:**
   * **100% Full Refund:** Order non-delivery, completely incorrect dish delivered, or delivery cancellation prior to food preparation.

3. **Dispute Resolution Ladder:** Tier-1 Customer Support (2 hours) $\rightarrow$ Operations Manager Review (24 hours) $\rightarrow$ Binding Arbitration in Ilorin, Kwara State under the Arbitration and Mediation Act 2023.

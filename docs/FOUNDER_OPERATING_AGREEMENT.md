## 23.2 Guiding Principles of Founder Governance

1. **Founders as Long-Term Stewards:** Founder equity represents long-term commitment. It is earned through multi-year execution, not merely initial concept origination.
2. **Universal Reverse Vesting:** Vesting applies strictly to all founders without exception.
3. **Explicit Decision Domains:** Operational, technical, and corporate decisions have unambiguous singular owners.
4. **Planned Deadlock Resolution:** Disagreements are anticipated and managed through a 4-stage escalation process.
5. **Clean & Fair Departures:** Departure is treated as an operational contingency with defined share-repurchase mechanisms.
6. **Primacy of the Corporate Entity:** In any conflict between individual founder interests and the fiduciary solvency of Dispatch Technologies Ltd., the company's long-term interests prevail.

## 23.4 Share Vesting & Acceleration Architecture

### Standard 4-Year Vesting Schedule with 1-Year Cliff
All ordinary shares held by founders are subject to monthly reverse vesting over **48 months**, commencing on the formal Incorporation Date:
* **Months 1–11 (The Cliff):** 0% vested. If a founder voluntarily resigns or is terminated for cause during this window, **100% of their shares are forfeited and returned to the corporate treasury**.
* **Month 12:** Exactly **25.0%** of total shares vest immediately.
* **Months 13–48:** Exactly **1/48th (2.083%)** of total shares vest on the final business day of each calendar month.
* **Month 48:** 100.0% fully vested.

### Acceleration Provisions
1. **Single-Trigger Acceleration (25% on Acquisition):** Upon a verified change of control (sale of $\ge 50\%$ of corporate voting shares or asset liquidation), exactly 25% of each founder's remaining unvested shares shall accelerate and vest immediately.
2. **Double-Trigger Acceleration (100% on Termination Post-Sale):** If within twelve (12) months following a change of control, a founder's employment is terminated without cause, or the founder resigns for Good Reason (material reduction in compensation, title, or relocation $> 100\text{ km}$ from Ilorin/Lagos), **100% of their remaining unvested shares accelerate and vest immediately**.
3. **Termination Without Cause Acceleration:** If the Board terminates a founder without cause, exactly **twelve (12) months of additional vesting accelerates**, accompanied by six (6) months of salary severance.
4. **Death or Permanent Incapacity:** 100% of unvested shares accelerate immediately, vesting to the founder's designated estate or next of kin. The company retains a 180-day Right of First Refusal (ROFR) to repurchase the shares at independent fair market value.

## 23.7 Founder Compensation, Dividend Policy & Expenses

* **Pre-Series A Salaries:** Founder base salaries are fixed at **₦600,000 NGN/month** each. This represents a ~50% discount to market rates, reflecting equity alignment.
* **Salary Adjustments:** Founder salaries cannot increase prior to Series A unless the company achieves two consecutive quarters of net operating profitability or an institutional funding round specifically allocates founder compensation increases (capped at ₦800,000 NGN/month pre-Series A).
* **Dividend Freeze:** Zero corporate dividends shall be declared or paid prior to Series A. All operating cash flows are reinvested into cluster expansion.
* **Corporate Expense Policy:**
  * Legitimate business expenses (economy domestic flights, hotel accommodation $< ₦50,000$/night, official client meals, ₦100,000/mo communication stipend) are reimbursable against original receipts.
  * Any single expense exceeding **₦200,000 NGN** or travel exceeding **₦500,000 NGN** requires written co-signature from the other founder.

## 23.8 Intellectual Property Assignment & Work Product Ownership

1. **Complete Assignment of Pre-Incorporation Work:** Each founder irrevocably assigns, transfers, and conveys to Dispatch Technologies Ltd. all right, title, and interest in and to any and all intellectual property created prior to incorporation relating to the business (including backend code, SQL schemas, Figma designs, business plans, financial models, and domain names).
2. **Work-for-Hire Doctrine:** All software, algorithms, Gemini prompts, Gemma datasets, documentation, and customer databases authored by founders during their tenure constitute "works made for hire" and remain the sole property of the company.
3. **Post-Departure Data Purge:** Upon departure, founders are legally required to delete all source code, system credentials, and confidential records from personal devices and certify compliance in writing.

## 23.9 Confidentiality, Non-Compete & Non-Solicitation Covenants

* **Confidentiality:** Strict non-disclosure of platform proprietary data (financials, merchant lists, driver rosters, algorithms, AI prompts) surviving for **five (5) years** post-departure (and perpetually for trade secrets).
* **Targeted Non-Compete (12 Months):** For twelve (12) months following termination, a departed founder shall not directly or indirectly launch, operate, advise, or invest in any on-demand food delivery or motorcycle logistics platform within the Federal Republic of Nigeria.
* **Non-Solicitation of Personnel (18 Months):** Departing founders shall not solicit, recruit, or hire any current employee or contractor of Dispatch Technologies Ltd.
* **Non-Solicitation of Merchants & Riders (12 Months):** Departing founders shall not solicit partner food canteens or verified dispatch riders to terminate their commercial relationship with the platform.
* **Remedies for Breach:** Immediate forfeiture of unvested equity, mandatory company buyback of vested equity at 50% of fair market value, and entitlement to injunctive relief.

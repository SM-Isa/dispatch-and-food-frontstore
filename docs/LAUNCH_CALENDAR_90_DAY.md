## 24.8 Contingency Pivot Playbook

If real-world operations diverge from assumptions by Day 60, activate these pre-engineered recovery protocols:

* **Pivot A: Weak Customer Demand ($<100$ orders/day, CAC $> ₦3,500$):**
  * *Diagnosis:* Value proposition friction or excessive delivery tariff.
  * *Intervention:* Conduct 20 structured in-person customer interviews; implement a subsidized ₦500 delivery tariff for 14 days; pivot marketing from broad social ads exclusively to hyper-dense WhatsApp campus groups and hostel gate table-talkers.
* **Pivot B: Supply Resistance (Vendor Churn $> 25\%$, Active Merchants $< 80$):**
  * *Diagnosis:* Commission resistance or tablet operational complexity.
  * *Intervention:* Temporarily lower vendor take rate to 12% for 30 days; replace tablet software with 100% WhatsApp text notifications; CEO personally meets with the top 20 merchant matriarchs to renegotiate menu exclusivity terms.
* **Pivot C: Fleet Attrition & Rider Churn ($> 35\%$/month, Delivery Latency $> 50\text{ min}$):**
  * *Diagnosis:* Low rider daily net earnings or excessive deadhead miles.
  * *Intervention:* Increase flat rider delivery payout from ₦580 to ₦650; introduce a ₦1,500 daily bonus for completing 8 deliveries; hold an emergency town hall with ACOMORAN union heads to resolve local fuel depot disputes.
* **Pivot D: Cash Depletion Threat (Cash $< ₦20\text{M}$ at Day 60):**
  * *Intervention:* Immediately freeze all non-essential hiring; reduce paid social marketing to zero; syndicate an internal ₦15M convertible bridge note among existing angel investors; shift ops exclusively to self-sustaining Tanke and GRA clusters.
